## Three-position VPN sheet, paired VPNs back as two rows, clearer Split tunneling and steadier Windows connections — changes since 360

- **VPN sheet with three positions.** The pull-up VPN sheet on Home stops compact, in the middle (just above the protocol button) and at full height under the VPN ON/OFF switch, on every platform, and scrolls to the very last row.
- **Paired VPNs shown as two rows again.** A paired VPN is displayed as two separate rows, as in 1.2.80, and customers who 1.2.82 moved to the wrong row are moved back automatically.
- **Clearer Split tunneling.** One Split tunneling concept with country flags for every destination, consistent wording in all 12 app languages.
- **Steadier connections on Windows.** ULTRA no longer gives up on a quiet carrier port too early (Windows carrier timeouts), and false clock-skew errors are gone; the Apple ULTRA framework is rebuilt from the same source.
- **Easier payment.** The promo code is kept through checkout, a repeated charge is prevented, and guest prices and promotions come from the server.
- **Includes 1.2.82 (367).** All changes of 1.2.82 (367) — redesigned VPN sheet, automatic reconnection and macOS On-Demand stability, Android split-tunneling presets, Windows installer compatibility — are part of this release.
