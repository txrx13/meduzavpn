# MeduzaVPN 1.2.43 (246) — Windows update

A Windows-only update of the graphical app, on top of **[1.2.43 (245)](1.2.43-245.md)**. Other platforms stay at their current builds.

## What changed on Windows

- **The connect button tells the truth during an outage.** When the connection to the server drops, the button now shows "Connecting…" while the app reconnects by itself, and turns back to connected when it recovers. Before, it stayed on "connected".
- **No DNS around the tunnel.** While ULTRA is connected, name lookups go only through the VPN; the computer's own resolver (a home router, the provider's DNS) is not asked, not even while the tunnel reconnects. Split tunneling rules by domain keep working right after a reconnect.
