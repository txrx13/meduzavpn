## Changes

- Corrected home and Connect screen layouts on compact screens, including accessible controls and localized connection/traffic labels in left-to-right and right-to-left languages.
- Corrected ULTRA recovery across engine restarts and stale connection state.
- Kept the home-screen VPN ON/OFF indicator under the settings gear and the main ring as the connection control.
- Payment outcomes are checked against the order; if a payment method fails, the payment sheet offers another method. SHARED welcome offers display the SHARED price.
- The iOS candidate includes adaptive outer/inner screen layouts and a half-open layout separating the connection controls from the VPN list. Its availability is subject to the Apple publication steps above.
- The tvOS candidate includes home-screen corrections and an updated ULTRA core. No performance or device-specific outcome is claimed by these notes.

Earlier features and installation instructions remain in the [README](../README.md) and [historical release notes](1.2.57-322.md). This release does not announce new macOS functionality.
