## Changes

- Home and Connect controls remain accessible on compact screens; connection and traffic labels follow the selected language, including right-to-left layouts.
- Corrected ULTRA connection recovery and stale state across engine restarts.
- The home screen keeps the VPN ON/OFF indicator under the settings gear, with the main ring as the connection control.
- Unclear payment outcomes are checked against the order. A failed payment method offers another method, and SHARED welcome offers display the SHARED price.
- The iOS build includes adaptive outer/inner screen layouts; a half-open layout separates the connection controls from the VPN list.
- The tvOS build includes home-screen corrections and an updated ULTRA core.

Earlier features remain in the [README](../README.md) and [historical release notes](1.2.57-322.md). macOS remains outside this rollout.
