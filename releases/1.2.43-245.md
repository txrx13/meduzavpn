# MeduzaVPN 1.2.43 (245) — Windows update

A Windows-only update of the graphical app. It carries everything in **[1.2.43 (244)](1.2.43-244.md)** plus the fixes below. Other platforms stay at their 244 and 242 builds.

## What changed on Windows

- **Split tunneling "direct" rules work.** Connections a rule sends directly (for example "local sites go direct") left through the tunnel's own routes and looped back into it, so those sites did not open. They are now bound to the computer's real network interface.
- **A dropped tunnel comes back by itself.** After a network change or a server restart the app used to stay "connected" with no internet until the VPN was switched off and on. It now shows "connecting", keeps all traffic inside the VPN meanwhile, and reconnects on its own.
- **Sessions last longer than a day.** The app renews the ULTRA profile in the background; on Windows the renewed profile was not applied, so the first reconnect after 24 hours could fail. It is applied now.
- The ULTRA engine for Windows is rebuilt from the current core.
