# MeduzaVPN 1.2.43 (247) — Windows update

A Windows-only update of the graphical app, on top of **[1.2.43 (246)](1.2.43-246.md)**. Other platforms stay at their current builds.

## What changed on Windows

- **Uninstalling no longer stops at an error.** Removing MeduzaVPN showed "The specified service does not exist as an installed service" and waited until it was clicked away; a silent uninstall waited forever. It now finishes by itself in seconds.

Checked on a clean Windows 11 machine with this release: install, sign-in, MeduzaVPN ULTRA, MeduzaVPN, Hysteria 2, OpenVPN and Outline all connect and carry traffic through the VPN; split tunneling sends "direct" sites past the VPN; DNS stays inside the tunnel; uninstall completes.
